PM Internship App
Government Scale • Mobile App

Bridging the gap between students and government internships.

The Problem: Students struggled to find verified government internships due to scattered information and complex application flows. First-time users often dropped off before completing their profile.

My Approach: I prioritized "Progressive Disclosure"—breaking the heavy onboarding into bite-sized steps. I designed a card-based listing system that filtered noise and highlighted eligibility criteria upfront.

Outcome: Reduced perceived cognitive load by simplifying navigation paths. Design focused on high-contrast readability and thumb-zone accessibility for rural connectivity contexts.

UX Optimization • Concept

Fixing the "Seat Selection Anxiety" in movie apps.

The Problem: Existing apps have cluttered seat selection screens. Users often lose context of which seat they selected or how much it costs until the final checkout, causing drop-offs.

My Approach: Designed a persistent "Summary Dock" that stays visible during selection. Simplified the color coding for accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA compliant) and reduced the booking flow from 5 steps to 3.

Key Iteration: Moved from a grid list to a visual theater layout after testing showed users preferred spatial representation over lists.
